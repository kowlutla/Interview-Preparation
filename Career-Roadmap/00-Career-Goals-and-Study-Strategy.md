- [ ] Follow the numbered files in sequence, adjusting based on target job descriptions.

4. Microservices, distributed systems, Kafka, caching, and resilience.
5. HLD and LLD, with regular case-study practice.
6. Git, build tools, testing, CI/CD, containers, cloud, and production engineering.
7. Spark, Avro, Iceberg, and other role-specific topics based on target job descriptions.
8. Resume, project deep dives, behavioral preparation, company research, and mock interviews.
