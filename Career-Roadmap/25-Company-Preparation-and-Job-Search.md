# Company Preparation and Job Search

- [ ] Calibrate target roles, seniority, and expectations from job descriptions
- [ ] Build a skill-gap matrix for each target role
- [ ] Research product, business model, engineering culture, and technical stack
- [ ] Verify company-specific interview stages and senior-level assessment areas
- [ ] Adapt DSA practice and system-design preparation to the role and level
- [ ] Build a focused outreach plan for referrals, recruiters, and relevant connections
- [ ] Prepare for recruiter screens and explain experience, scope, and motivation
- [ ] Evaluate compensation, equity, benefits, growth, and offer terms
- [ ] Search remote-first/remote-friendly roles and verify India-based work eligibility
- [ ] Consider time zones, work authorization, notice period, joining date, and competing offers
